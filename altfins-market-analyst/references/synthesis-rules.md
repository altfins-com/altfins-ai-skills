# Synthesis Rules

Use these rules when turning validated altFINS-facing evidence into an analysis output.

## Fact vs inference separation

Always separate:

- validated facts from the source or returned data
- your interpretation of those facts
- open questions that the available evidence does not settle

## Evidence ordering

Preferred evidence order:

1. market or setup context
2. supporting indicator or technical evidence
3. recent signals, if relevant
4. news or event context, if relevant

This keeps the analysis traceable and prevents news commentary from overpowering the technical evidence.

## Uncertainty labeling

Use direct uncertainty labels when needed:

- validated fact
- reasonable inference
- open question
- missing input

Do not hide uncertainty behind confident wording.

## What not to imply

- do not imply future performance is confirmed by technical evidence
- do not imply causality from news unless the link is directly supported
- do not imply a market move is guaranteed because multiple signals align
- do not turn analysis into personalized financial advice

## Context handling

News and event context can strengthen or weaken a setup narrative, but should usually be framed as context or interpretation unless the evidence is direct and validated.

## Preferred ending

Finish with one of these:

- a short risk-aware outlook
- a list of open questions
- the next best checks to run
