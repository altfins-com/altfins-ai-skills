# Analysis Workflow

Use this workflow when the user wants structured altFINS-backed market analysis.

## Step 1: Frame the question

Capture the minimum research brief:

- asset or asset universe
- timeframe or interval
- research goal
- desired output shape

If any of these are missing, ask for them or clearly state the assumption.

## Step 2: Choose the evidence family

Map the question to one or more validated families:

- Screener for market-wide filtering and idea generation
- Technical Analysis for curated setup views
- OHLCV Data for price structure and candle history
- Analytics History for indicator or metric time series
- Signal Feed for recent signal-driven triggers
- News or Calendar Events for context

## Step 3: Gather evidence in layers

Use a stable evidence order when possible:

1. market context
2. indicator or setup evidence
3. recent signal evidence
4. news or event context

This keeps the final synthesis easier to audit.

## Step 4: Write the analysis conservatively

Preferred output sections:

- Objective
- Validated facts
- Interpretation
- Open questions
- Suggested next checks

## Step 5: Avoid overclaiming

- Do not present probable explanations as confirmed facts.
- Do not imply an MCP capability unless the tool was actually discovered or documented.
- Do not turn market analysis into personalized financial advice.
